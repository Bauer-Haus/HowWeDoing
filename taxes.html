What the states tax

No state raises revenue the same way. Nine levy no individual income tax at all and lean on sales or property taxes instead; others do the reverse. The measure that matters for a household is not any single rate but the total burden — all state and local taxes paid as a share of income.

The total burden

State and local taxes paid by residents as a share of their income. This counts every tax a household actually pays, including taxes paid to other states.

Reading these rates. A top marginal income rate applies only to income above the top bracket threshold, so few households pay it on most of their earnings. States shown with a 0% corporate rate may levy a gross receipts tax instead — Ohio, Texas, Nevada and Washington all do. Washington taxes capital gains above a threshold at 7% despite having no wage income tax. Rates are 2026; burdens use the latest available year.
