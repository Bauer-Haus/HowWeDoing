Sources and methodology

Every number on this site comes from a published federal or non-partisan source. This page names the source and vintage for each series, states what the figures can and cannot support, and explains how to refresh the data.

Read this before quoting a figure. The series here are drawn from different agencies, surveys and reference periods, and were compiled into a single snapshot. Where a number matters — a tax rate you will act on, a statistic you will publish — check it against the primary source linked in the table below, which is always more current than this snapshot. Rankings are more robust than individual values: a state that ranks 3rd on household income will rank near there in any vintage, even if the dollar figure has moved.
