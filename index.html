    About the numbers. Every figure here is compiled from published federal and non-partisan sources — BEA, BLS, the Census Bureau, the FBI and the Tax Foundation — and each series carries its vintage on the sources page. Series are drawn from different years and surveys, so a state's income figure and its GDP figure may not share a reference period. Read the methodology before quoting a number.
